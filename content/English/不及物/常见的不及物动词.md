以下是一些常见的不及物动词分类及示例：

### 一、表示动作或状态的不及物动词

- **run**（跑）：He runs every morning.

- **swim**（游泳）：She swims in the lake.

- **sleep**（睡觉）：We sleep at night.

- **laugh**（笑）：They laughed loudly.

- **cry**（哭）：The baby cried for milk.

- **smile**（微笑）：She smiled at me.

### 二、表示发生、出现的不及物动词

- **happen**（发生）：What happened yesterday?

- **occur**（发生，出现）：An idea occurred to him.

- **take place**（发生）：The meeting took place in the hall.

- **appear**（出现，显得）：A rainbow appeared in the sky.

- **disappear**（消失）：The bird disappeared into the forest.

### 三、表示存在、保持的不及物动词

- **exist**（存在）：Does life exist on Mars?

- **remain**（保持，残留）：Little remains of the old building.

- **stay**（停留，保持）：Please stay here.

- **lie**（躺，位于）：He lies on the sofa. / The town lies by the river.

- **stand**（站立，位于）：She stands at the door. / The tower stands on the hill.

### 四、表示变化的不及物动词

- **grow**（生长，变得）：Plants grow in spring. / He grew angry.

- **change**（变化）：The weather changes often.

- **improve**（改善）：His health improves day by day.

- **increase**（增加）：The population increases rapidly.

- **decrease**（减少）：The number decreases slowly.
