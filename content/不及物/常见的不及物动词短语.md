以下是一些常见的不及物动词短语分类及示例：

### 一、表示动作趋向的不及物动词短语

- **come up**（出现；走近）：A new idea came up in the meeting. / She came up to me and said hello.

- **go away**（离开）：Please go away; I need to be alone.

- **run out**（用完；耗尽）：Our time ran out before we finished the task.

- **move on**（继续前进；继续前行）：Let's move on to the next topic.

- **break down**（出故障；崩溃）：The car broke down on the way. / He broke down when he heard the news.

### 二、表示状态变化的不及物动词短语

- **turn up**（出现；调高）：He turned up late for the party. / Please turn up the radio.

- **die down**（逐渐减弱；平息）：The wind died down at midnight.

- **get up**（起床）：I get up at 6 o'clock every day.

- **fall asleep**（入睡）：She fell asleep as soon as she lay down.

- **come true**（实现）：His dream of being a doctor came true.

### 三、表示发生、进行的不及物动词短语

- **take place**（发生）：The sports meeting will take place next week.

- **break out**（爆发；突发）：A fire broke out in the building. / War broke out between the two countries.

- **go on**（继续；进行）：The meeting went on for three hours.

- **come about**（发生；产生）：How did this accident come about?

- **carry on**（继续）：Carry on with your work; don't stop.
